Requests up to 240 FPS initially, then the highest frame rate advertised by the camera. A preferred 640 × 480 resolution reduces processing cost; the browser chooses the actual mode. Camera settings are not measured throughput. Average FPS measures analyzed frames over elapsed time; min/max/latest are completed roughly one-second sampling windows, including stalls. The target is 12% of the shorter video dimension. Brightness is mean Rec. 709 weighted RGB luma (0–255), not calibrated light intensity. Keep the page visible and use good lighting: exposure, device temperature, browser scheduling, and resolution can limit throughput. This measures browser-delivered frames, not the sensor's theoretical maximum or necessarily the performance of a native Android application. Video callbacks can also be limited by the display refresh rate.
